About SP WorkTrack

SP WorkTrack is an in-house digital production control platform developed to improve production visibility, process discipline, traceability, accountability, and operational decision-making across the shopfloor.

The system digitally tracks production activities, machine-wise progress, manpower activity, quality checkpoints, work completion status, deviations, and operational records in a structured and traceable way.

SP WorkTrack is designed as a practical step toward lean manufacturing, digitization, operational transparency, and future Industry 4.0 readiness by converting shopfloor activities into measurable and actionable production data.

System Evolution Roadmap

Level 1 — Production Entry System

Digital replacement of manual production recording with structured production entry, machine selection, shift-wise tracking, and standard vs actual time capture.

Level 2 — Production Tracking System

Expanded machine-wise and department-wise tracking with work progress visibility, machine status monitoring, and remaining vs completed work control.

Level 3 — Production Control & Traceability System

Current SP WorkTrack system level focused on production control, quality integration, accountability, and centralized digital traceability.

Level 4 — Production & Performance Management System

Current SP WorkTrack system level focused on machine dashboard visibility, standard vs actual monitoring, quality and rework visibility, machine status control, team activity foundation, and performance review support.

Level 5 — Production Planning & Decision Support System

Planned capability for manpower planning, overtime estimation, workload balancing, capacity analysis, and planning support.

Level 6 — Production Intelligence System

Long-term vision involving predictive analytics, intelligent production insights, automated alerts, and AI-assisted operational decision-making.

Current System Position

SP WorkTrack v 2.0 currently operates at the Production Performance Management stage, providing strong operational visibility, quality integration, process accountability, and structured digital production records.

The current backend and data structure already create a strong foundation for future productivity analytics, planning systems, and intelligent manufacturing support.
