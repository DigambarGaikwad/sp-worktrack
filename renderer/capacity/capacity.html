Capacity Planning

This module is planned for future SP WorkTrack versions and will focus on machine loading, manpower requirement, overtime planning, workload balancing, and advance production decision support.

Machine Loading

Planned vs available machine capacity visibility based on standard time, remaining work, and machine status.

Manpower Planning

Future visibility of manpower requirement by department, shift, workload, and production priority.

Overtime Requirement

Estimated overtime requirement based on remaining work, available capacity, and planned delivery targets.

Planning Support

Foundation for workload balancing, priority planning, bottleneck visibility, and future decision support.
